        Get a reminder

        DCAS does not send an alert when an exam opens or closes, so this site publishes the dates as a calendar instead. Subscribe once and every application period appears in the calendar you already use, opening and closing dates both, with a reminder three days before an exam closes. It keeps updating on its own when DCAS extends a deadline.

        There is nothing to fill in. The events are already written. Nobody is asked for an email address and nothing about you is stored, because the reminder comes from your own phone or laptop rather than from us.
